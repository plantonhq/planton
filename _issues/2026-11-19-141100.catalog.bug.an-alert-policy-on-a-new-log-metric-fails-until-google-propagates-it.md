# An alert policy on a log metric created in the same deploy fails for minutes

**Area**: `catalog/gcp/gcpmonitoringalertpolicy`, `catalog/gcp/gcplogmetric` (its `catalog.md` advises deploying both together)
**Type**: bug. Handed by the burn-down of every reported issue (2026-09-25, Harsha's H14-3, the catalog half).

## What a platform engineer sees

A `GcpMonitoringAlertPolicy` that filters on a `GcpLogMetric` created in the same deploy fails with Google's 404 for up to about ten minutes, until Google propagates the new metric. The log metric's page tells authors to deploy both together, which is exactly what fails.

## Fix

Correct the guidance on both pages: a new metric is not usable at once, and the alert policy's apply succeeds when re-run. Then weigh a bounded wait in the alert policy's modules if both engines can express one. A runner-wide retry for known transient errors is the platform's to design, not this issue's.
