# KubernetesNeo4j should refuse a ServiceMonitor on the community edition

**Area**: `catalog/kubernetes/kubernetesneo4j` (`spec.proto` validation)
**Type**: feature (validation; the field comment now says it, the spec does not enforce it)

## What a platform engineer sees

`service_monitor_enabled: true` with `edition: community` installs cleanly and renders a ServiceMonitor, but Neo4j serves Prometheus metrics in the enterprise edition only (Neo4j Operations Manual, "Expose metrics -- Enterprise Edition", read 2026-10-04: `server.metrics.prometheus.enabled`, default port 2004). On community the target never answers, and the monitoring stack's `TargetDown` posts about it ten minutes later.

## Suggested fix

A CEL rule on the spec: `!this.service_monitor_enabled || this.edition != 'community'`, with a message that names the enterprise requirement and points at watching the pod's readiness instead. A spec test for both arms. The field comment already carries the fact (2026-10-04).
