# The GCP catalog can't declare a folder-level or billing-account-level IAM grant

**Area:** the open-source GCP catalog (`catalog/gcp/`), IAM grant kinds.
**Confidence:** read in the catalog: `GcpFolder`'s own comment says a folder's grant "belongs on its own additive resource", and no such kind exists. A live proof met the gap on 2026-11-18.

## What happens

A platform engineer composing a landing zone grants roles on folders and on billing accounts all the time. The catalog has project-level grant kinds (`GcpProjectIamMember`), but nothing that grants on a folder or a billing account. So two things can't be built from the catalog:
- `GcpOrgPolicy`'s permission is granted only above a project (`GcpRolesGrantedAboveAProject`). Its live proof needs a grant on the catalog lanes' sandbox folder, and the estate allows no hand grants.
- `GcpBillingBudget`'s proof needs `roles/billing.costsManager` on the billing account. The founder approved that grant on 2026-11-17, and no kind can declare it.

## Fix

Add two additive grant kinds on the pattern of `GcpProjectIamMember`: `GcpFolderIamMember` (folder, role, member) and `GcpBillingAccountIamMember` (billing account, role, member). Each needs its own `permissions.yaml`, with `reach: organization` or `billing_account`. Then the estate can declare the test lanes' folder and billing grants, and `GcpOrgPolicy` and `GcpBillingBudget` can be proven live.
