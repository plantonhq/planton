# Copywriting Stage Area

One dated folder per change to what the site says, `YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>/`, holding:

- `draft-N.md`: the words a page will say, every sentence beside what backs it (a chapter and proof index, a positioning key, a pricing or counts constant, a public document), the claim check, and what the two cold reads changed. A later draft is a new file; earlier drafts stay.
- `handoff.md`: the routes, where the copy lives in `src/data/`, the argument, the component mapping, the revisions the independent review forced, what the page must not claim, and how it was verified.

There are no HTML previews. The built page under the capture harness (`make capture`) is the preview, and the independent reviewer grades pixels. The folders are the site's copy history; nothing here is deleted.
