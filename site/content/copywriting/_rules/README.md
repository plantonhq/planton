# Copywriting Rules

Two rules, one workflow, for changing what planton.ai says.

- `update-planton-ai-copy-writing.mdc`: from feedback or a product change to a dated draft in `../_stage-area/` where every sentence sits beside the chapter or document that backs it, a claim check, two cold reads (the named visitor, the copywriter), a story correction first when the draft finds the story overclaiming, and a `handoff.md`.
- `implement-planton-ai-copy-writing.mdc`: from the handoff to a data edit in `src/data/`, a green `make build`, captures compared against the build before, the independent design reviewer's PASS, and the record.

The words live in data and pages render them; a claim typed into a component is a defect. The site `README.md` names every law the words obey.

`pdf_converter/` turns feedback PDFs dropped in `../_workspace/` into Markdown so a session can read them.
